# AAF (2019) team logos: derived crops

No official source with separate AAF team logo files survives. ESPN never carried the league, CBS's AAF pages were never
archived, and the Wayback Machine holds only the shutdown page of aaf.com. That page shows one image of all eight logos:
`https://aaf.com/images/TeamLogos.png`, 836x64 px, archived at
`https://web.archive.org/web/2019id_/https://aaf.com/images/TeamLogos.png`.

These PNGs are **crops of that strip** at its native 64 px height. They are cut at the columns in `crops.csv`, found from
the image's transparency, one per logo, in the strip's alphabetical order. The owner approved them on 2026-09-30. The
manifest marks them `source=aaf-strip-crop` and `variant=crop_64px`, and captures the untouched strip as its own row, so
nothing derived passes as an original. `pff_franchise_id` is PFF's AAF franchise id (719-726).
